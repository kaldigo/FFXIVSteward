# Steward

Companion owns classification, collection references, sale rules and market coordination. AutoRetainer owns existing disposal and storage workflows. Preserve other plugins' configurations; import read-only. Default dry-run. Exact item IDs, dyes ignored, both native plates and Glamaholic, level-one glamour gear, nonmarketable blues and special/unknown acquisition sources preserved until collected. Marketable blues are not protected by the blue-rarity rule; other protection rules still apply. Marketability means item eligibility for marketboard trading, not whether listings or fresh prices exist. Confirm exact collected copies before surplus disposal. Unknown is never zero or absent. User exclusions override all handling. Keep native acceptance distinct from automated validation.

Execution requires live readiness, protocol 2, fresh protection imports and current source fingerprints. Stale populated collection sets never authorize disposal. Managed discard is denied. Do not delegate managed bulk operations without a per-item submission gate. Persist uncertain-run holds and leave automatic off after clearing one. Only release a proven owned handoff; expiry recovery must not unlock another plugin's work. Keep the last-copy omission active across live refreshes.
